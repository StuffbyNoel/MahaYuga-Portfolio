THE CONCEPT

MahaYuga is a 3D animated piece focused on atmospheric storytelling, dramatic lighting, and expressive motion. Every shot was crafted to convey scale and mood through deliberate camera choreography and detailed environment design.

The production pipeline spanned concept blocking, modeling, texturing, rigging, animation, and final compositing—balancing stylized visuals with physically grounded lighting and reflections.
